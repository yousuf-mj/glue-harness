# Tone: casual

For: Slack messages, meeting invites, quick emails to people I work with.

- **Shape:** no summary block and no headings. One line of context, then the ask or a few bullets. This replaces the section 2 layout.
- **Length:** fits on a phone screen without scrolling. If it doesn't, it's probably a neutral-tone update.
- **Contractions:** always (I'm, it's, we'll).
- **Exclamation marks:** at most one.
- **Greeting and sign-off:** from the voice file.
- **Still clear:** one ask per message. Say what you need, and by when if it matters.
- **Varied copies:** when the same message goes to several people, vary the wording so it doesn't read as a mail merge, but keep the content the same.
