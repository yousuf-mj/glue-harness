# Tone: formal

For: policies, proposals, decision records, and anything for leadership, customers or people outside the company.

- **Body is neutral:** the rules and the content have no personality. Third person for rules ("Engineers must..."), "we" for decisions the team or company made.
- **Optional intro in my voice:** at most 3 lines at the top, for context or why it matters, clearly from me. The body never switches back to it.
- **Must / need / can:** exactly as section 3 defines them. A policy uses "must" only for a real requirement.
- **No negative contractions** (cannot, do not). Other contractions only in the intro.
- **No** chatty words, emojis or exclamation marks.
- **Every claim** has a source, a named owner or a date.
